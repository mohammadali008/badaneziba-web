# Anime waxing film · production notes

Character artwork: OpenAI built-in ImageGen, generated for this project. The final original sprite sheet is `anime-wax-sprites.png`. No CLI/API-key fallback was used.

Final image prompt:

> Create a production-ready ANIME CHARACTER ANIMATION SPRITE SHEET for a tasteful men's waxing salon landing page, not a mockup. Use case: illustration-story. Output a square 2 by 2 sprite sheet with FOUR equal cells, transparent alpha background, no grid lines, no panels, no lettering, no labels. In each cell show the SAME friendly adult man age about 35 from head to waist, front view, centered at exactly the same position and scale. Premium clean modern anime cel animation, beautiful clean outlines, warm tan skin, dark short swept-back hair with a few silver strands, neat beard, relaxed masculine physique, dark charcoal trousers at the waist. Bare upper chest is tasteful nonsexual salon treatment imagery. His expression is happy, confident and gently smiling in EVERY frame. Important comparison: viewer's LEFT half of his chest has clearly visible natural dark chest hair; viewer's RIGHT half of his chest is clean and smooth after waxing, with a neat off-white wax strip being held just off the smooth upper chest by his right-side hand. Clear readable split down the center of chest, same in all four frames. No wounds, no redness, no dramatic muscles, no erotic pose. Both shoulders, all of head, both elbows and hands entirely INSIDE each cell with generous 10 percent transparent margin so animation sprites do not touch or merge. The other arm is relaxed and bent at his side. Four closely matching successive animation keyframes, consistent anatomy and costume: top left eyes open smiling with waxing strip at chest; top right smiling eyes half closed and strip lifted very slightly away from chest; bottom left smiling closed eyes and strip slightly raised; bottom right smiling eyes open with strip returning towards original position. Small natural breathing variation only, no camera motion, no change in pose scale or face identity. Use warm highlights, charcoal outlines, subtle coral-orange rim lighting matching a black-and-orange luxury website. This is ONE consistent character drawn four times for a looping hand-drawn anime clip, NOT four different men. No logos, no watermarks, no text.

Motion: four original drawings assembled with held cel timing, gentle breathing and small highlights. `scripts/render_anime_film.py` produces a silent 12-second, 24-fps, 1280×960 H.264 MP4 and a WebP poster. This is an illustrated motion clip rather than live footage of a client or an actual treatment demonstration.
